# Sorted Sheets Calculators

Free UK money calculators: care home fees, inheritance tax gifts and the 7-year rule, Christmas savings,
debt snowball v avalanche, and Gift Aid. Static pages, no cookies, no tracking: everything is worked out in the browser.

This repo is the published copy. The source, figures (with gov.uk sources and dates) and tests live in the
private Sorted Sheets repo under `site/`; `site/public/` is copied here to publish. For information only,
not financial, legal or tax advice.
