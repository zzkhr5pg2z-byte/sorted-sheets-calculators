# Sorted Sheets

The Sorted Sheets website: a shop window for the Sorted Sheets Etsy shop (SortedSheetsShopUK), free UK money
calculators (care home fees, inheritance tax gifts and the 7-year rule, Christmas savings, debt snowball v avalanche,
Gift Aid, business mileage and Self Assessment), custom spreadsheets, and a page for creators. Every Buy button goes to
that product's own Etsy listing. Static pages, no cookies, no tracking: the calculators work everything out in the browser.

This repo is the published copy. The source, figures (with gov.uk sources and dates) and tests live in the
private Sorted Sheets repo under `site/`; `site/public/` is copied here to publish. For information only,
not financial, legal or tax advice.
