Examples of what I can build

Real workbooks, shown with made-up example data. Yours is built around what you keep track of.

Staff Rota & Wages

Cafés, shops and salons with 3 to 15 staff

  • Type in the week's shifts: hours and wage cost work out per person and per day
  • Flags anyone paid below the legal minimum for their age
  • Counts each person's holiday left automatically
  • Shows the week's wage cost against your budget

Quotes & Invoices

Plumbers, electricians, joiners and painters

  • Pick a job number and a print-ready quote or invoice fills itself in
  • Labour at your hourly rate, markup on materials, VAT if you're registered
  • Says what each job is waiting on: chase payment, send the invoice, follow up
  • Win rate, money owed and overdue jobs at a glance

Stock & Reorder Tracker

Cafés, bakeries, small shops and market stalls

  • Log deliveries, sales and waste: current stock works itself out
  • Builds the order list by supplier and says if you're short of their minimum
  • Shows waste in pounds and the five items wasted most
  • Turns a shelf count into the exact adjustment to log

Appointments & Takings

Hairdressers, barbers, nail techs, groomers, tutors

  • The next 7 days in time order, with a reminder text ready to send
  • Takings by month, split by cash, card and transfer
  • Shows what no-shows cost and which clients to win back
  • Catches double-bookings and appointments left unpaid

Club Subs & Membership

Sports clubs, youth clubs, choirs and community groups

  • Works out what each member owes on monthly, termly or yearly fees
  • A who-owes list that keeps itself up to date, with a reminder text for each person
  • Money in by month and by how people paid
  • Flags payments that don't match a member, so the totals always add up

Jobs & Mileage Log

Cleaners, gardeners, dog walkers and mobile trades

  • Works out the HMRC mileage claim for every job, 55p and 25p split for you
  • Lists who still owes you and flags anything over 30 days
  • Shows what you earn per hour from each client
  • Month by month: jobs, hours, money in and miles

Childminder Hours & Invoices

Childminders and after-school clubs

  • Log times in and out: hours work out, rounded to your charging block
  • Funded hours come off automatically, with the amount to claim from the funder
  • Pick a child and a one-page invoice lists every session, ready to print
  • Shows who has paid, part paid or still owes this month

Landlord Rent & Arrears

Landlords with 1 to 10 properties

  • Rent due counted from each tenancy's own dates: monthly, weekly or 4-weekly
  • Shows straight away who is behind and by how much
  • Costs by property and type, with profit before tax and yield
  • Flags gas, electrical, deposit and insurance dates that are overdue or due soon

Daily Till Cash-Up

Cafés, shops, bars and takeaways

  • Count the notes and coins: it gives the takings and what to leave for tomorrow's float
  • Flags days the till is over or short by more than you allow
  • Spots card payments rung through as cash
  • Shows your busiest and quietest days, this month or across the year

Recipe & Menu Costing

Cafés, takeaways, home bakers and cake makers

  • Costs every dish from your ingredient prices, per portion
  • Gives the exact price to charge to hit your target food cost
  • Type a price rise for an ingredient and see which dishes go over target
  • Handles VAT properly, including zero-rated takeaway bakes

Fundraising & Gift Aid

Small charities, churches, PTAs and community groups

  • Progress to your target, after event costs and with Gift Aid included
  • Works out Gift Aid on every gift, and says why when a gift doesn't qualify
  • Builds the list of gifts to claim, with the donor details HMRC asks for
  • Money raised by event, by month and by how people gave

Holiday Let Bookings & Cleaning

Holiday cottages and Airbnb or Booking.com hosts

  • Flags double-bookings, but not same-day changeovers
  • A cleaning list in date order: next arrival, guests and hours to clean
  • Month calendar with each guest's name and the cleans
  • Occupancy, platform fees, cleaning and profit by property and month
