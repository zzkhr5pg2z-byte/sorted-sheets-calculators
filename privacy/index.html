Privacy and cookies

No cookies or tracking unless you say yes to one thing: the Pinterest Tag. Here's what it does and how to change your mind.

The short version

  • Nothing from Pinterest loads unless you press Accept on the cookie banner.
  • The calculators work everything out in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent anywhere, whatever you choose.
  • You buy on Etsy, not here. Etsy's own privacy policy covers that.

Who runs this site

Sorted Sheets, a small UK spreadsheet business (see About). Questions about your data: email sortedsheetsuk@gmail.com.

The Pinterest Tag, only if you accept

Sorted Sheets advertises on Pinterest. The Pinterest Tag is a small piece of code from Pinterest that tells it which ads bring people to this site and whether they go on to buy, so the ads can be shown to people more likely to want a spreadsheet, and the ones that don't work can be stopped.

If you press Accept, your browser loads the tag from Pinterest and it reports:

  • each page you look at here, and on a product page, which template it is;
  • when you click through to a template on Etsy: which template and its price;
  • what you type in the shop's Find box.

Pinterest sets cookies to do this (on this site they start with _pin or _epik), and if you use Pinterest it can link the visit to your Pinterest account. It doesn't get your name, email or payment details from this site. Pinterest decides how it uses what it collects: see Pinterest's privacy policy, which also explains how to turn off ads based on what you do on other sites.

If you press No thanks, or never press anything, the tag never loads.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to see the choice again. If you change from Accept to No thanks, the tag stops, its cookies on this site are removed and the page reloads. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings works too, and you'll be asked again on your next visit.

Your choice is kept in your browser's own storage on this device, not in a cookie, so you aren't asked on every page. It isn't sent anywhere.

Buying on Etsy

Every Buy button goes to the template's listing on Etsy. Etsy takes the payment and sends the download, so your order, address and payment details are handled by Etsy under Etsy's privacy policy. Sorted Sheets sees your order in its Etsy shop, as every Etsy seller does.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what data is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Sorted Sheets keeps nothing about you from this site; for the Pinterest Tag, Pinterest holds the data. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
