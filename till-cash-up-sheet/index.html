Till Cash Up Sheet, Daily Cash Up Spreadsheet UK, Till Float and Takings Tracker, Over Short Log, Cafe Shop Bar, Excel & Google Sheets

Cash up the till every night without a calculator. Type how many of each note and coin is in the drawer and it gives you the cash takings and exactly what to leave in for tomorrow's float. Then log the day: the till's end-of-day readings, the cash you counted and the card machine total. It works out the cash and card over or short and flags the day Short, Over or OK against the allowance you set.

Start Here shows the month at a glance: takings, the card and cash split, over/short, big shorts, your average day, best day, paid outs and the cash waiting to be banked. Monthly and By Weekday show 12 months of takings and which days are quiet.

  • Cash Count: Count the notes and coins: cash takings and tomorrow's float
  • Daily Log: One row a day: till readings, cash counted, card machine, over/short and a flag
  • Monthly: 12 months of takings, cash, card, paid outs, banking and over/short, with a chart

What it does

  • Tomorrow's float made up from what's in the drawer, or how much change you're short
  • Daily Log: till readings v cash counted and card machine, with cash and card over/short worked out
  • Short / Over / OK against your own allowance, and a count of days short by more than your 'big short' level
  • Spots a card payment rung through as cash (cash short, card over by the same amount)
  • Paid outs (milk, the window cleaner) still count as takings; cash banked and cash waiting to be banked
  • 12 months of takings, cash, card, paid outs, banking and over/short, with a chart and the best day each month
  • Average takings for each day of the week, for a month or the whole 12 months; 500 days of rows
