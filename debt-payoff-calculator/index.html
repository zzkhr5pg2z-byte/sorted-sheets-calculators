      How it works

      Both methods pay the minimum on every debt each month and put everything extra on one "focus" debt. When that one is cleared, its whole payment rolls on to the next. What changes is the order:

      • Snowball clears the smallest balance first. You see debts disappear sooner, which keeps a lot of people going.
      • Avalanche clears the highest interest rate first. It usually costs less in interest, sometimes by a lot when one card has a much higher rate.

      The calculator keeps your total monthly payment the same the whole way through, adds interest each month at the APR divided by 12, and counts the months until every balance is £0. If two debts tie, the one higher up the list goes first. "At the minimum only" is how long each debt takes if you only ever pay its minimum.

      Its limits

      • Lenders work out interest in their own ways (often daily), and APRs can include fees, so real figures will be a little different.
      • It assumes the minimum payment stays the same. On many credit cards it falls as the balance falls, which makes paying only the minimum take much longer.
      • It doesn't include new spending, fees, or 0% deals ending.
