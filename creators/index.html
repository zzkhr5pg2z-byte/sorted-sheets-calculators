UK creators who post about:

The offer

You get a free copy of any Sorted Sheets template to try. No strings attached: you don't have to post about it, review it or say anything nice about it.

If you like it and want to share it, it works as affiliate marketing. You get your own discount code to give your audience, and a share of every sale that uses it.

What the templates are

Spreadsheets that do the sums for you, for Excel and Google Sheets, set up in pounds. You type in your own numbers and the totals, plans and charts work themselves out. They're sold as instant downloads in the Sorted Sheets shop on Etsy. A few of them:

  • Christmas Budget & Gift Planner

    A gift list by person with budget and actual cost, planned v spent for food, decorations and cards, and how much to put aside each week until Christmas. £6 on Etsy.

  • Monthly Budget Tracker

    Plan each category, fill in what you actually spent and see what's left. Comes in a classic and a pastel version; the pastel one adds a progress bar for each category and a chart. £6 on Etsy.

  • Complete Money System

    Type each payment in once and the dashboard, monthly budget, 12-month view, savings goals and debt tracker all update from it. £19 on Etsy.

  • Savings Goals & 52-Week Challenge

    Up to 10 savings goals, each showing what's left to save and how much to put aside each month to hit its date, plus the 52-week savings challenge. £5 on Etsy.

  • Meal Planner & Grocery List

    Pick the week's meals from your own recipes and it writes the shopping list, takes off what's already in the cupboard, rounds up to packs and estimates the cost from your own prices. £9 on Etsy.

  • UK Sole Trader Bookkeeping Kit

    Income and expenses in HMRC's self-employment categories, mileage at the simplified rates, use of home, and a 6 April to 5 April summary with your profit. £15 on Etsy.

Every other template in the shop is on offer too.
