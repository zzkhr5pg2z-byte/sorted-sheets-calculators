Staff Rota Template UK with Wages, Weekly Rota Spreadsheet, Minimum Wage and Holiday Tracker, Cafe Shop Salon, Excel & Google Sheets

Plan the week's shifts and see the wage bill before you put the rota up. Type start and finish times and unpaid break minutes for up to 15 staff, and it works out each person's hours and gross pay, the hours and wage cost for each day, and what's left in your wage budget.

It checks every hourly rate against the legal minimum for that person's age band (the April 2026 rates and the April 2027 rates already announced: the week's date picks the right ones), flags long shifts without a proper break and under 18s over their limits, and keeps track of each person's holiday left.

What's inside (5 tabs)

  • Start Here: The week, your wage budget, six live totals, day by day and the minimum wage rates
  • Staff: Each person's age band, hourly rate, usual hours, pay check and holiday left
  • Rota: The week's shifts: hours, wage cost and checks for each person and each day
  • Holiday: Holiday taken or booked, in hours
  • Guide: How it works, in plain English

What it does

  • Weekly rota for up to 15 staff: start, finish and unpaid break, including shifts past midnight
  • Hours and gross wage cost per person, per day and for the week, against your wage budget
  • Minimum wage check by age band: 21 and over, 18 to 20, under 18 and apprentice
  • April 2026 and April 2027 minimum wage rates built in, picked by the week's date
  • Break check: 20 minutes on shifts over 6 hours; under 18s 30 minutes over 4.5 hours, and their 8 hours a day and 40 a week limits
  • Holiday: 5.6 weeks of each person's usual hours (capped at 28 days), less holiday taken or booked, for each person's own holiday year
  • Busiest day, and how many staff are on each day
