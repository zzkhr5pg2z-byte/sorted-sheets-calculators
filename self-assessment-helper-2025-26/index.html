What it does

  • Expenses in the SA103S categories (boxes 11 to 19), with a business use % for things like your phone
  • Simplified mileage at the 2025/26 rates: 45p a mile for the first 10,000 car or van miles, then 25p; 24p on a motorcycle
  • Income Tax by band for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or the Scottish rates if you lived in Scotland
  • Type the pay and tax from your P60, so your bands are right if you have a job or pension too
  • Class 4 National Insurance (6% and 2%), and where you stand on Class 2
  • Payments on account: whether you need them, the first due 31 January 2027 and the second 31 July 2027
  • A Your Return tab with the figure for each box to copy
  • A checklist of what you need before you file (UTR, Government Gateway, P60 and more), key dates, a countdown and the late-filing penalties
  • Flags rows with no date, or dated outside 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026

What it doesn't cover

Savings interest, dividends, rental income, capital gains, Student Loan repayments, the High Income Child Benefit Charge, pension or Gift Aid relief, capital allowances, or more than one business. It doesn't file anything with HMRC.
