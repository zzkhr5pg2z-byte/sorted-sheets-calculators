Lots of subjects, dozens of topics and not enough time. Type in your exams and your topics once, rate how confident you feel about each one, and this spreadsheet tells you what to revise first.

Shaky topics, topics you haven't looked at for a while and topics with an exam coming up rise to the top. It counts down to every exam, helps you plan your week around the hours you actually have, keeps your study streak, and works out the grade for each past paper from the grade boundaries you type in.

What's inside (9 tabs)

  • Start Here: Your study hours, eight live numbers, your next exams and shakiest topics
  • Subjects: Type each subject once: exams, topics, confidence and hours for each
  • Exams: Countdown for every paper, next-up order, clash check, everything still to come
  • Topics: Confidence 1-5, last revised, revise-by date, status and a priority score
  • This Week: The 10 topics to revise first, a Monday-Sunday planner and hours by subject
  • Study Log: Every session, totals by week and subject, your streak and a chart
  • Past Papers: Score, %, v target, the grade from that paper's boundaries, up or down
  • Progress: Pick a subject: its last 10 papers and a chart; latest grade for every subject
  • Guide: How it works, in plain English

What it does

  • Countdown to every exam, in date order, with morning or afternoon and calculator or not
  • Flags two exams in the same session
  • Spaced repetition: a revise-by date for each topic from your confidence (you can change the gaps)
  • A priority score for every topic, and the 10 to revise first this week
  • Monday-Sunday planner from dropdowns, checked against the hours you set, with suggested hours per subject
  • Study log with totals by week and subject, a streak count and a chart
  • Past papers: %, v your target, the grade from the boundaries you type, and up or down since your last paper
  • Works with letter grades (A*-G, including C*) or numbers (9-1): you type the grades
