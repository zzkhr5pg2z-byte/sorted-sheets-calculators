How much can I claim for business mileage?

If you're self-employed and use simplified expenses, you claim a flat rate for each business mile instead of your vehicle's running costs. Type your business miles to see the claim at HMRC's 2026/27 rates.

For the whole tax year, or one trip.
In the same car or van, since 6 April. Leave at 0 for a whole year.

How it works

Flat rates per business mile, 2026/27
VehicleRate
Car or van, first 10,000 business miles55p
Car or van, after that25p
Motorcycle24p

Miles are counted from 6 April each tax year. The trip that takes you past 10,000 miles is split: the miles up to 10,000 at the higher rate, the rest at the lower one.

  • The flat rate replaces fuel, insurance, repairs, servicing, road tax and MOT for that vehicle. You can still claim parking and tolls.
  • Only business journeys count, not your normal commute.
  • You can't use flat rates for a vehicle you've already claimed capital allowances on, and once you use them for a vehicle you keep using them.
