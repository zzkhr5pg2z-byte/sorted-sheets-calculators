About Sorted Sheets

A small UK spreadsheet business: templates that do the sums for you, free money calculators, and custom spreadsheets.

Who runs it

Sorted Sheets is a small UK spreadsheet business started by a sixteen-year-old. It makes spreadsheets that do the sums for you, for Excel and Google Sheets, and sells them on Etsy. The calculators on this site are free. Each one links to a matching spreadsheet: that's how Sorted Sheets earns from them.

How buying works

This site is a shop window. Every Buy on Etsy button goes to that template's own listing in the Etsy shop, SortedSheetsShopUK. Etsy takes the payment and sends the download, so you never pay on this site.

How the figures are checked

Each calculator does the same sums as one of the Sorted Sheets spreadsheets. Automatic tests give the website and the spreadsheet the same examples and check they get the same answers.

Every limit and rate was checked in November 2026 against gov.uk and, where gov.uk doesn't publish it, the official site that does (nhs.uk, gov.wales, nidirect.gov.uk and legislation.gov.uk). The figures are for the 2026/27 tax year and are checked again each April. Each calculator lists its sources.

Not advice

The calculators and spreadsheets are for information and planning. They aren't financial, legal or tax advice, and they can't take everything about your situation into account. For big decisions, speak to the council, HMRC, a solicitor or a qualified adviser.

Your privacy

There are no cookies, no analytics, no adverts and no sign-up. The sums are done in your browser, so nothing you type is sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host GitHub may log basic technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

Spotted a mistake?

Please say through the Sorted Sheets shop on Etsy. Corrections are made as quickly as possible.
