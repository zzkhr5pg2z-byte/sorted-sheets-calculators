Got one to ten rental properties and a pile of bank statements? Add each property's rent once, then log payments as they come in. This spreadsheet counts the rent due from each tenancy's own dates, monthly, weekly or every 4 weeks, and shows straight away who's up to date and who's behind, and by how much.

Costs go in by property and type, so you see profit before tax and yield for each one, and the Reminders tab flags gas, electrical, deposit and insurance dates before they're due.

What's inside (7 tabs)

  • Start Here: Six live totals and each tenant's status
  • Properties: Up to 10 properties: rent due, received and arrears
  • Rent In: Every rent payment
  • Costs: Every cost, by property and type
  • Property Summary: Profit and yield per property, and month by month
  • Reminders: Check and renewal dates, flagged when due

What it does
