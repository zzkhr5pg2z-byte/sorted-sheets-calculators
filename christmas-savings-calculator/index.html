How it works

Still to pay is your budget less what you've already spent. Take off what you've saved, and what's left is the amount still to find. That's divided by the number of whole weeks until your deadline.

Weeks are counted down, not up: 20 days to go is 2 whole weeks, not 3. With less than a week to go it counts as 1 week. The weekly amount is rounded up to the next penny, so you won't come up a few pence short.

If part of Christmas is going on a credit card or a pay-later plan, add it in the box for that. You'll see a second weekly figure that clears that too, so it's not waiting for you in January.

Making it easier

  • Set up a weekly standing order on payday into a separate savings pot, so the money is out of sight.
  • Start in January next time: spread over a whole year, the weekly amount is much smaller.
  • Write the budget per person before you shop, and keep track of what each present actually cost.
